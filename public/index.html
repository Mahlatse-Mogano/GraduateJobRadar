How listings are checked. Every morning the radar collects new adverts from SETA, government and youth-opportunity websites, then opens each official link. Listings that have closed, been removed, or are only general information pages are left off.

No closing date shown? The advert didn’t give one. Open the link and check the closing date and requirements before you apply.

Province filters also show listings that don’t name a province, since many are national. Industry tags are added automatically, so check the advert itself.

Listings may be out of date or incomplete; always confirm details with the provider. Learnership Radar isn’t linked to any SETA, employer or government department. This site collects no personal information and uses no tracking.
